01 / Field guideThe rules, in plain sight +

Move wheneverHold A/D or left/right arrows to run; Space, W or up arrow jumps. You can run while airborne and pass through the opponent.

AttackHold J to swing every 0.65 seconds. Each swing costs 18 stamina. Reach is 1.25 horizontal and 0.8 vertical units; jumping can evade a swing.

GuardHold K or Shift. Guard halves movement speed, drains 8 stamina/second, reduces damage to 4 and counters for 6 if you have 8 stamina.

RecoverRelease guard to recover 12 stamina/second. Jumping costs 12 stamina. Release and press jump again after landing.

Jev's clockAbout two snapshots per second, one request at a time. AI controls expire after 0.7 seconds. Responses older than 1.2 seconds are ignored; physics always uses current positions.

Pause & touchPress Play to start, P or Esc to pause. Switching away pauses automatically. Hold the on-screen buttons on touch devices. No requests are made while paused.

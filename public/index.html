01 / Field guideThe rules, in plain sight +

One turn, two choicesSelect Stay, Left, Right or Jump. Then click Attack or Defend to commit. The AI gets the same choices.

MovementLeft/right costs 5 stamina and moves one of seven lanes. Stay is free. Fighters cannot share a lane.

JumpCosts 15 stamina. Dodge ground strikes until your next turn. Land for one turn before jumping again.

AttackCosts 20 stamina after movement. Reach: one horizontal lane. Ground damage: 24. Jump damage: 16, and can hit downward. Out-of-range swings miss but still cost stamina.

Defend & counterRestore 25 stamina and reduce incoming damage to 6. Counter ground attacks for 12 damage if you have 10 stamina to spend. Jump attacks avoid counters.

Keep it simpleNo potions or random damage. Health/stamina cap at 100. Zero health ends the battle; 50 rounds is a draw. Mode switching preserves the battle.

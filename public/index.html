01 / Field guideThe rules, in plain sight +

Attack At distance 1, spend 20 stamina to deal 24 damage (6 against a guard).

Defend Restore 25 stamina. Guard until your next action, reducing the next opponent turn’s damage.

Approach / Retreat Move one step closer / farther and recover 10 stamina. Distance stays between 1 and 3.

Heal Use your one potion to restore 30 health, up to 100. No stamina cost.

Turn order You act, then the opponent. No dice or critical hits. Zero health ends the battle; 50 rounds is a draw.

Compare modes Switch at any time between turns. The battle state stays the same; the next decision uses the selected engine.

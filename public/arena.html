How to playRules and controls

Move anytimeHold A/D or the arrow keys to run; Space, W or ↑ jumps. You can steer in the air and pass through the opponent.

AttackHold J to swing every 0.65 seconds. Each swing costs 18 stamina. Reach is 1.25 horizontal and 0.8 vertical units, so jumping can dodge a swing.

GuardHold K or Shift. Guarding halves your speed, drains 8 stamina a second, cuts damage to 4 and counters for 6 if you have 8 stamina.

RecoverRelease guard to recover 12 stamina a second. Jumping costs 12 stamina. Release and press jump again after landing.

Jev's timingAbout two snapshots a second, one request at a time. Each move lasts up to 0.7 seconds, and answers older than 1.2 seconds are ignored.

Pause and touchPress Play to start, P or Esc to pause. Switching tabs pauses too. On touch screens, hold the on-screen buttons. Nothing is sent while paused.

Jev decides from a snapshot of the fight. Physics always uses the live positions.
